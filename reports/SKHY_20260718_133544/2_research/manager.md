The July 29 earnings catalyst is a binary event with high whisper numbers.

The tie-breaker: the structural AI demand thesis is powerful enough to warrant exposure, but the cyclical and technical risks demand disciplined sizing rather than a full conviction Buy. Building a position gradually allows participation in the upside while preserving capacity to add if the bear's $132-135 target materializes.
