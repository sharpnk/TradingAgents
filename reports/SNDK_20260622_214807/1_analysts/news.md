Druckenmiller's move from GOOGL to SNDK, STX, MU, AVGO, and ARM reflects a thematic rotation:
- **AI Software** (high multiples, crowded) → **AI Hardware** (memories, storage, networking, semis)

4. **Geopolitical hedge** — The Iran situation creates tail risk. Memory stocks have shown resilience so far, but a sustained Strait of Hormuz closure would be risk-off for all equities.
5. **Watch the yield curve** — The 10Y-2Y spread has collapsed from 0.49% to 0.27% in one month. A further flattening into negative territory would historically signal recession risk and be negative for high-multiple stocks.
