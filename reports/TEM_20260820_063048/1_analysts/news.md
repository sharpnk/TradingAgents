# News & Macro Research Report — TEM (Tempus AI, Inc.)
**Analysis date:** 2026-08-20 | **Exchange:** NMS (NASDAQ) | **Sector:** Healthcare / Health Information Services (AI-enabled precision oncology & diagnostics)

---

## 1. Company-Specific News Flow (TEM) — Last Week & Rolling 6 Weeks

**Catalyst #1 — Moderna-Merck cancer vaccine clears late-stage trial (Aug 18–19):** TEM rallied alongside BNTX, MRVI, RGEN, NVAX as investors connected the positive clinical readout to Tempus's planned **$1.5B all-stock acquisition of Personalis (PSNL)**, announced July 20. The deal gives Tempus control of Personalis's **NeXT Personal MRD (minimal residual disease) test** — a cancer-recurrence monitoring technology whose relevance is amplified by the immunotherapy/vaccine wave. 24/7 Wall St. framed the deal as "looking brilliant" post-trial.

**Catalyst #2 — Q2 2026 earnings (reported ~July 30):** Revenue **$382.49M (+22% YoY)**, a beat vs. estimates (+0.4% revenue surprise, +69.2% EPS surprise), and a swing from a **-$42.84M net loss a year ago to +$5.64M net income** — first quarterly profit. Management **raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $1.60–1.61B** (~25% growth), citing accelerating oncology testing volumes, data/AI demand, and FDA-linked pricing gains.

**Catalyst #3 — FDA approval for xT CDx:** Zacks estimates the approval could unlock **~$85M in annual revenue** from higher pricing/reimbursement, with "bigger gains tied to xF" — a durable reimbursement growth driver.

**Ongoing strategic overhang — Personalis acquisition:** All-stock, ~$16.25/share (~5.6–6% premium). Morgan Stanley likes the strategic fit (strengthens precision oncology); Needham flagged the premium as above peers; both stocks initially sold off on integration concerns. Pro forma, TEM gains full ownership of the MRD test it had been distributing, targeting a cancer-monitoring market.

**Counterweights / risk signals:**
- **CEO insider sale:** Founder-CEO sold 250,000 shares (~4% of indirect stake) via pre-scheduled plan; retains ~6.5M shares. Pre-scheduled, but optics matter.
- **Valuation & cash burn:** Zacks and Simply Wall St. both flag high valuation + integration risk despite improving growth.
- **Institutional support:** Cathie Wood/ARK bought **$8.7M of TEM** after a 14% pullback (pre-earnings); TEM is an ARK flagship holding.
- **Sector catalyst ahead:** 24/7 Wall St. (Aug 11) flags **Anthropic's September IPO with a healthcare-AI pitch** as a potential sector re-rating catalyst for names like TEM.

- **Volatility:** VIX **15.84** (down ~7% over the month) — risk-on regime, supportive of growth equities.

## 4. Actionable Insights for TEM Traders

1. **Bullish idiosyncratic news flow:** Q2 beat + first profit + raised guidance + FDA xT reimbursement (~$85M/yr) + Moderna-Merck vaccine validation of MRD positioning + potential Anthropic IPO sector halo. Near-term news catalysts skew positive.
2. **Macro headwind to watch:** 10Y at 4.71% and an oil-driven inflation scare (WTI→$90 with 68% probability in August) argue against chasing extended multiple expansion; rate cuts are priced out (86% no-cut in 2026).
3. **Event calendar:** Personalis deal integration updates, xF reimbursement news, and the September Anthropic IPO are the next potential re-rating catalysts.
4. **Key risks to monitor:** CEO 10b5-1 selling continuation, all-stock deal dilution/integration execution, cash burn, and any risk-off shock from Hormuz/oil.
