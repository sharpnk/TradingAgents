**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Both sides made their strongest case, and the honest read is that the bull won the argument on the company while the bear won the argument on the current price.

The bull is right about the fundamentals. LITE just printed roughly $1.0B in quarterly revenue (+109% YoY), expanded gross margin to 48%, guided to ~$1.275B next quarter (+27% sequentially), and has delivered six consecutive quarters of revenue acceleration. Free cash flow has been positive for three quarters even while capex roughly doubled, and the balance sheet is materially stronger. The AI-optical demand driver is real and structural — copper cabling is the documented bottleneck, and hyperscalers are capacity-shopping, not price-shopping. That is not the old telecom cycle, and the bear never truly refuted the P&L momentum.

But the bear is right about the price. At ~$926, LITE trades around 27x trailing revenue and roughly 700x trailing free cash flow; even the bull's generous forward FCF case leaves it at 100x+. The stock is ~45% above its 200-day SMA, the 50-day is still flat-to-turning, there is a hard resistance shelf at $957-974 only about 4% above, and — most telling — the bull's own execution plan calls for entries 8-12% lower into the $855-815 zone. Add a binary NVDA print, peers selling off on good news, and 8:1 bullish sentiment with visible froth, and the immediate risk/reward at $926 is not good enough to chase.

So this is not an "avoid forever" call, and it is not an unqualified Buy. It is a name we want exposure to, built on the terms both sides effectively converged on: constructively but patiently. That is why I land on Overweight rather than Buy or Hold.
