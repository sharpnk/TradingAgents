However, the bearish risks are real and structural — the 47% PT cut from Phillip Securities, the H1 2026 worst-performer status, and the complete absence of Reddit discussion all indicate the stock lacks broad-based positive momentum. The sector rotation out of software is a macro headwind that can override company-specific stories in the short term.

The **Mildly Bullish** rating reflects that the balance of high-quality signals (insider buying, HSBC upgrade, retail dip-buying) slightly outweighs the bearish signals, but not enough to be strongly confident. The stock's position near a 52-week low with improving product momentum creates a potential entry setup for patient traders, but the path to re-rating requires the AI disruption narrative to be convincingly disproven — something that may take multiple quarters to play out.
