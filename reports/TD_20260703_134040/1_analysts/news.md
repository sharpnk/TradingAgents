## 1. Company-Specific Developments

### Record Q2 Earnings & Dividend Hike
TD reported **record Q2 earnings** across all major operating segments — Canadian Personal & Commercial Banking, Wealth Management, Insurance, and Wholesale Banking — each reaching all-time highs. OSFI's reduction of the capital buffer is a direct catalyst. It enables TD to deploy excess capital toward lending growth, M&A, or shareholder returns — particularly relevant given the bank's recent record earnings momentum.

With a 2.80% yield, the post-Q2 dividend hike, and record profitability, TD offers a compelling **total return profile** for income-oriented investors. The bank's ability to grow its dividend is supported by strong capital ratios and the regulatory buffer cut.
