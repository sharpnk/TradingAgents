2. HBM is contractually sold out through 2027—this provides earnings visibility that commodity DRAM never had.

The bull's rebuttal relies on "this time is different" and extrapolating unsustainable growth rates. The correct posture is to acknowledge that AI has structurally improved MU's earnings power while recognizing that the current run-rate cannot persist.
