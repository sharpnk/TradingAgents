## 1. Company Profile & Business Overview

Cellebrite DI Ltd. is a global leader in **digital intelligence software**, providing law enforcement, government agencies, and enterprises with tools to collect, review, analyze, and manage digital evidence from mobile devices and cloud sources. The company transitioned to the public markets via a SPAC merger (2021) and trades on the Nasdaq under CLBT. Its business is characterized by:

- **Subscription/recurring revenue model** with very high gross margins (~82–84%)
- Deep exposure to **government & public-safety customers** (ethical/regulatory risk factor)
- Expansion into **enterprise** and **cloud-based investigative workflows** (Cellebrite Cloud, Guardian, Inseyets)
- A **net-cash balance sheet** with zero traditional debt (only capital-lease obligations)
