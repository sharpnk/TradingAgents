## 1. Company Overview & Business Model

Circle Internet Group is the issuer of **USD Coin (USDC)**, the second-largest USD stablecoin globally. Its business model is interest-rate-driven: Circle earns most of its revenue from **interest income on the reserves backing USDC** (predominantly short-dated US Treasuries and cash), plus transaction/distribution fees. This is a "capital-markets infrastructure" business whose economics are tightly linked to (a) the stock of USDC in circulation and (b) prevailing short-term interest rates.

## 7. Risks & Weaknesses
1. **Valuation risk:** ~56x forward EPS and ~8.3x sales. Forward EPS of $1.59 implies the market expects ~70% EPS *decline* from TTM — i.e., consensus already discounts heavy normalization. Any disappointment is punished in a high-multiple name.
2. **Growth deceleration:** Revenue has been flat-to-down for three consecutive quarters (peak Q4'25 $770M → Q2'26 $701M); TTM growth has collapsed from +64% to ~+6%.
3. **Falling-rate sensitivity:** Lower interest rates compress reserve yields and, therefore, gross profit (the core revenue engine). Gross margin (~22%) has held so far, but a continued easing cycle is a direct headwind.
4. **Reserve outflow risk:** Q2'26 restricted cash fell -4.6% QoQ — the first decline. Continued outflows (Tether competition, DeFi rotation, or bank-run dynamics) would directly shrink revenue.
5. **Thin current ratio (1.035):** The model depends on perfect reserve/liability matching; an extreme redemption wave would stress liquidity (though reserves are liquid Treasuries).
6. **Earnings volatility:** Fair-value swings on investments, tax items, and SBC cause large quarterly EPS swings (e.g., -$4.48 → +$0.64 EPS between Q2'25 and Q3'25) — low earnings predictability.
7. **Concentration:** Revenue tied to one product (USDC) and one macro variable (short rates).
