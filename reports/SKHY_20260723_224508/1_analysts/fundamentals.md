## 1. Company Overview

SK hynix Inc. is a South Korean multinational semiconductor supplier and a dominant global player in memory chips (DRAM and NAND flash). The company is a critical supplier to the AI ecosystem through its High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) solutions, which are essential for AI accelerators (GPUs from NVIDIA, AMD, etc.). The company trades on the NASDAQ via SKHY.

## 8. Key Risk Factors

1. **Cyclicality of Memory Market:** Semiconductors, especially memory, are notoriously cyclical. The current AI-driven boom could moderate.
2. **High Debt-to-Equity (13.28):** While improving, leverage is still elevated compared to pre-downturn levels.
3. **High Beta (2.027):** The stock is 2x more volatile than the market — significant downside risk in a market correction.
4. **Customer Concentration:** Heavy reliance on a few large AI customers (NVIDIA, etc.) for HBM revenue.
5. **Geopolitical Risk:** Exposure to US-China tensions and South Korea's geopolitical situation.
6. **Competition:** Samsung and Micron are aggressively competing in the HBM space.
7. **Capital Intensity:** Heavy ongoing CapEx requirements to maintain technological leadership.
