## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW & PROFILE

Amazon.com, Inc. is the world's largest internet retailer by revenue and market capitalization. The company operates through three primary segments: **North America**, **International**, and **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. AWS dominates cloud computing globally. Amazon also generates revenue through digital advertising, Prime subscriptions, third-party marketplace services, and physical stores (Whole Foods, Amazon Fresh).

**Overall Assessment:** Amazon is firing on all cylinders. Revenue growth is consistent (11-12%), but the real story is the **profitability transformation** — going from a $2.7B loss in 2022 to a $77.7B profit in 2025, with margins continuing to expand. The massive CapEx spending ($131.8B in 2025) is a double-edged sword: it suppresses near-term FCF but positions Amazon for dominant AI/cloud growth. With $143B in cash and a reasonable 27.9x P/E for a company growing earnings at 30%+, the fundamental picture remains very constructive.

**FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: HOLD** — The fundamentals are exceptionally strong, supporting a long-term bullish view. However, the stock is near its 52-week high, FCF is compressed by heavy CapEx, and the recent significant debt issuance warrants monitoring. For traders, the current level (~$233 near the 200-day MA) may present a better entry point than chasing highs, but the underlying business quality is excellent for long-term positions.
