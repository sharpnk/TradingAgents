## 7. Growth Drivers & Risks

### Growth Catalysts

1. **GLP-1 Franchise (Mounjaro/Zepbound):** The primary growth engine, with explosive revenue growth from tirzepatide for diabetes and obesity. This drug class has blockbuster potential exceeding $50B+ annually.
2. **Pipeline Strength:** Ongoing R&D ($13.3B annualized) in Alzheimer's, immunology, and oncology.
3. **Manufacturing Expansion:** Massive capex investments positioning LLY to meet surging GLP-1 demand and capture market share.
4. **Operating Leverage:** As revenue scales rapidly, fixed costs are being spread over a larger base, expanding margins.

### Key Risks

1. **Competition:** Novo Nordisk (semaglutide) and emerging oral GLP-1 competitors could erode market share.
2. **Pricing Pressure:** Regulatory scrutiny on drug pricing, particularly for high-revenue drugs like Mounjaro.
3. **High Valuation:** At ~42x TTM earnings, the stock leaves little room for earnings disappointment.
4. **Debt Load:** $43.4B in total debt could become a concern if interest rates remain elevated or if growth slows.
5. **Manufacturing Scale-Up Risks:** The massive capex program carries execution risk.
