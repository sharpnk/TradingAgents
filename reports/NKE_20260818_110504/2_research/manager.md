Meanwhile, the tape is unambiguously bearish: NKE is at a 12-year low, below all major moving averages, broke down on the heaviest volume in months, and bounced on weak volume with no confirmed reversal. Sector and consumer data reinforce caution. On balance, the bear's trend and cash-flow evidence outweigh the bull's promising but unconfirmed one-quarter inflection, so the right stance is to reduce exposure rather than accumulate or fully exit.
