1. **The squeeze is real but extended.** Two consecutive days with >150M shares and +20–30% gains is a high-conviction reversal; however, price is 23–31% above VWMA/10 EMA and above the upper Bollinger band. **Chasing at $2.43 is poor risk/reward** — statistically, extreme extensions fade. 3. **Key downside reference levels.** The breakout's integrity rests on holding above the **Aug 6 close ($2.02)** and, critically, the **Aug 6 intraday low ($1.63)**. A close back below $2.02 would negate much of the reversal; below $1.63 the squeeze thesis collapses.

Position sizes must be cut to account for ~10% daily ATR; stop levels should be sized off ATR, not arbitrary percentages.

5. **Momentum divergence caution.** RSI has rebounded to 56 but MACD remains negative. This profile (negative MACD, price below both SMAs, RSI recovery) has historically been associated with sharp but unsustainable relief rallies in high-short-interest names. Treat this as a **trade, not an investment thesis**, until the 50 SMA is reclaimed.

6. **Watch for a volatility contraction signal.** After the crash, ATR has been declining (0.33 → 0.24). A further contraction with price holding $2.00+ would build a base; an ATR expansion to the upside on a third high-volume day would signal a continuation squeeze.

## 5. Risk Factors

- **Structural downtrend intact:** price is ~49% below the 200 SMA and ~22% below the 50 SMA; both averages still falling.
- **Extreme extension:** 30%+ above the 10 EMA and above the upper Bollinger band invites a sharp mean-reversion.
- **Macro/high-short-interest fragility:** two-day +55% moves on >350M shares often reverse violently when buying exhausts.
- **Illiquidity tail risk:** at ~$2.43 with ~10% daily ATR, gap risk through stops is material; overnight gaps in either direction can be 15–25%.

## 6. Recommendation

Given the extreme two-day extension, still-negative MACD, and unresolved medium/long-term downtrend, the disciplined stance is **HOLD** for those already long (with a 2× ATR trailing stop near $1.95 and invalidation below $1.63), and **no new chase entries** at $2.43.
