## 1. Company Profile

TransMedics Group, Inc. is a commercial-stage medical technology company that developed the **Organ Care System (OCS)** — the first portable warm-perfusion and assessment system for donor organs (heart, lungs, and liver). The OCS platform enables "donation after circulatory death" (DCD) organ recovery and expands the usable donor pool, addressing a structural shortage in transplantation. The company is transitioning from heavy R&D/buildout into a commercial-scale manufacturing phase, as evidenced by the massive fixed-asset expansion in Q1 2026.
