## 8. Final Assessment

**NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)** continues to execute at an extraordinary level. The company has transformed from a gaming/GPU company into the undisputed leader of the AI infrastructure buildout. Key takeaways:

1. **Growth is decelerating but remains best-in-class:** Annual revenue growth went from +126% → +114% → +65%, but Q1 FY2026 still delivered 85% YoY growth. The base is now $81.6B per quarter.

2. **Margins are expanding:** The 71.5% net profit margin is unprecedented for a hardware-adjacent company, reflecting NVIDIA's pricing power and the value of its CUDA ecosystem.

3. **Valuation is compelling on a forward basis:** At 16.2x forward P/E and a PEG of 0.65, the stock is priced well below its growth rate — a classic sign of potential undervaluation for a growth company.

4. **Balance sheet and cash flows are pristine:** $68B net cash, $48.6B quarterly FCF, low debt, and aggressive buybacks provide a massive margin of safety.

5. **Key risks to watch:** Inventory build-up ($25.8B), potential AI CapEx cycle peaking, increasing competition, and geopolitical/tariff risks.

**For traders:** With a beta of 2.21, NVDA offers high-reward potential but comes with significant volatility. The stock's 52-week range ($164–$237) and current price (~$207, roughly mid-range) suggest the market is pricing in a balanced outlook. The forward P/E compression (32.6x TTM → 16.2x Forward) implies the market expects growth to continue but at a moderated pace.
