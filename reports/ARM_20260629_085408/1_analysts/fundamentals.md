## 1. Company Overview

Arm Holdings plc is the world's leading semiconductor intellectual property (IP) company. It designs the underlying architecture and instruction sets used by the vast majority of mobile processors globally and is rapidly expanding into data centers, automotive, IoT, and AI accelerators. The company operates an asset-light, high-margin IP licensing and royalty business model.

1. **Data Center CPUs**: Arm architecture gaining share in cloud data centers (NVIDIA, AWS, Ampere, Microsoft)
2. **AI/Edge Computing**: Arm's v9 architecture is well-positioned for AI inference at the edge
3. **Automotive**: Increasing share in ADAS and infotainment systems
4. **Higher Royalty Rates**: Transition from v8 to v9 architecture commands 2x+ royalty rates
5. **IoT Expansion**: The proliferation of connected devices drives long-term royalty growth

### Potential Headwinds
1. **Execution Risk**: Must deliver on the aggressive growth expectations baked into the stock price
2. **Competition**: RISC-V open-source architecture gaining traction as a potential long-term threat
3. **Geopolitical Risk**: China exposure and potential export control impacts
4. **Customer Concentration**: Heavy reliance on a few major licensees (Apple, Qualcomm, etc.)

The high beta (3.79) makes this a high-risk/high-reward proposition.
