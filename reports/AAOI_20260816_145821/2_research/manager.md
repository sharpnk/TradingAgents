**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: This was a genuinely hard-fought debate, and both sides landed real blows. The bull is right about the core inflection: AAOI is not the same business it was. Q2 revenue of $191.92M was up 86% YoY and 27% QoQ, 800G revenue is up roughly 10x, and the company has a multi-year Amazon supply agreement — a concrete validation from a brutal negotiator. The balance sheet was rebuilt ($499.7M cash, current ratio 2.78, low debt), net PP&E tripled to $772.3M, and Q2 operating cash flow swung from -$85.4M in Q1 to +$11.6M. The tape also matters here: this was not a single-stock meme bid. Lumentum +15%, Coherent +9%, Corning +5% — the entire AI-optics complex re-rated on confirmed hyperscaler capex, and AAOI is a high-beta pure-play on that theme. Price is above the 50-day and 200-day, MACD is on a fresh bullish crossover, and RSI at ~61 is not yet overbought.

But the bear's risk points are too good to ignore and they directly constrain how aggressively we should buy. The bull's valuation anchor — 32.7x forward EPS of $4.60 and a 0.78 PEG — is fragile. $4.60 on ~80.2M shares implies ~$369M of net income, which requires roughly a 31% net margin on $1.2B revenue; that is above AAOI's current 27.7% gross margin, and gross margin has actually been compressing (30.0% FY25, 29.1% Q1, 27.7% Q2) while operating losses widened. The profit bridge is a forecast, not a demonstrated trend. The stock has also been diluted ~180% cumulatively, H1 free cash flow was -$417.8M, insiders sold ~$120M against ~$1M of buys, and the price is +96% off the low in twelve sessions — extended above the 10-day by ~15% and hugging the upper Bollinger with an 11% ATR. The bear's own fair-value reference of ~$150.30 is essentially the current price: there is no margin of safety at $150.28.

The synthesis: the bear wins on "don't chase the vertical move," but the bear's own valuation says fair value is at the current price — that is a "no margin of safety" argument, not a "short it" argument. Meanwhile the bull wins on the underlying direction: revenue acceleration is real, the customer is real, the sector repricing is real, and the technical structure has turned constructive. When the trend is up and the debate is about price and pace rather than whether the growth is real, the disciplined answer is constructive exposure — but scaled, staggered, and risk-managed rather than a full-conviction Buy at an extended level. Hence Overweight: maintain/build a position, but do it with smaller size, defined levels, and respect for the event risk directly ahead.

**Strategic Actions**: Treat AAOI as a high-beta satellite/opportunistic position, not a core holding. Position sizing guidance consistent with Overweight: target roughly half to two-thirds of a normal full position initially, and only build to full size on confirmation.

Entry plan:
1. Starter: establish a reduced starter near current levels (~$150) only if the trader is underweight and can tolerate 11% daily ATR. Use half-size to keep stop distances survivable.
2. Preferred add: scale in on a pullback into the $130–134 confluence (10 EMA / 50-day SMA). This is the higher-quality entry.
3. Momentum add: add on a decisive close above $155.50 (upper Bollinger breakout), not before.

Risk management:
- Stop discipline below $127 (~1.3–1.5x ATR) on the starter; size so a stop-out costs a pre-defined, acceptable amount.
- Hard invalidation: a close below $110. Exit the position; the setup has failed.
- Given gap risk in an 11% ATR name, do not assume stops will fill exactly; use reduced size rather than tight stops.

Event risk (Aug 18 Rosenblatt, Aug 26 NVIDIA):
- Do not add size into these events. They are binary and largely anticipated.
- If the stock spikes into $172–180 on the catalysts without new fundamental confirmation (Amazon deal details, margin trajectory, or guidance raise), consider trimming into strength rather than chasing.
- If the CFO merely reiterates and the stock fades, do not panic-sell the starter; use the $134 (50-day) and $127 (stop) levels as the decision points.

Scale-up conditions — only grow toward a full position after at least one of:
- Gross margin stabilizes or turns back up (stops the 30.0→29.1→27.7 slide);
- A second consecutive quarter of positive operating cash flow;
- A pullback that resets the short-term extension (back into $130–134 and holding).

Re-evaluation:

- Reassess the entire stance on a close below the 50-day (~$134), with $110 as structural invalidation.
