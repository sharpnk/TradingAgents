## 8. Key Fundamental Drivers & Insights

1. **AI memory supercycle:** Enterprise SSD / NAND demand driven by AI training & inference storage, high-capacity drives, and data-center builds is the primary tailwind. Revenue growth of +175% YoY with 78% gross margins is consistent with tight supply and rising NAND contract pricing.
2. **Operating leverage:** Costs (R&D + SG&A ~$2.2B/yr) are relatively fixed; every incremental dollar of revenue drops to the bottom line. This explains the 60%+ net margins.
3. **Self-funded growth & deleveraging:** The company has gone from debt-funded (−$1.93B net income in Mar-2025 with a $1.83B impairment) to net cash with $3.7B on hand, all from operations.
4. **Asset-light model:** Capex of just ~1% of revenue means high FCF conversion, but dependency on external wafer supply (former-parent/JV agreements) is a structural constraint and margin risk if supply agreements are repriced.
5. **Forward expectations embedded in price:** Forward EPS of $265 implies the market is pricing ~$41–42B of forward net income — roughly 3.6x current TTM. This is aggressive and leaves little room for NAND price normalization.
