## 1. COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS

### 🏭 CHIPS Act Award & Texas Expansion (Major Catalyst)
On June 16, 2026, **Coherent signed a letter of intent** to receive up to **$50 million in direct CHIPS and Science Act funding** from the U.S. Department of Commerce. This funding is earmarked for expanding its **6-inch indium phosphide (InP) semiconductor facility in Sherman, Texas**. Key details:
- **Doubles** manufacturing space
- **Quadruples** wafer production capacity
- **Creates 1,000+ jobs**
- Deepens the already-strong **collaboration with NVIDIA** for AI datacenter optical networking
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang personally highlighted this facility as part of his thesis that AI will revive U.S. manufacturing

### 📉 The June 23 Optics Selloff – Valuation Reckoning?
On **June 23**, the photonics sector experienced a sharp, broad-based selloff:
- **COHR fell ~9%** to ~$387
- **AAOI (Applied Optoelectronics) fell 13%**
- **LITE (Lumentum) fell 8%**
- No company-specific negative catalyst for COHR — the move appeared to be **sector-wide profit-taking / valuation compression** after the group's massive YTD run
- **Partial bounce-back occurred overnight June 23-24** as AI/photonics stocks recovered some losses

### 🧠 JPMorgan Warning (June 11)
JPMorgan analyst Samik Chatterjee flagged a ~20% pullback from early-June highs, citing:
- **Limited summer catalysts**
- **Fears of delays in co-packaged optics (CPO) adoption**
- Called the setup "a buying opportunity" for long-term investors

### ⚠️ China Indium Export Controls – Supply Chain Risk (CRITICAL)
On **June 19**, Reuters reported that **China is tightening scrutiny over indium exports**. China produces ~70% of the world's indium, which is the **raw material for indium phosphide (InP)** — the very material Coherent uses for its high-speed optical chips. This presents a **dual-edged sword**:
- **Risk:** Input cost inflation or supply constraints if China imposes formal export controls
- **Opportunity:** Coherent's domestic Texas expansion (CHIPS-funded) positions it as a **critical onshore supplier**, potentially making it a **national security beneficiary**

### 📈 Long-Term Fundamentals Remain Strong
- **6-month stock rally: +121%** (peak ~$424)
- **Record backlog** with long-term customer commitments
- **NVIDIA investment** in the company's technology
- **Deleveraged balance sheet** improving financial flexibility
- However, **shareholder dilution** has been cited as a concern from the expansion funding
