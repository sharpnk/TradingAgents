The Consumer Price Index edged down to **332.568** in June from **333.979** in May, a **-0.42% decline** in the two-month window. Stood at **130.082** (May data), flat over the window. Core inflation remains sticky but not accelerating.

Unemployment fell to **4.2%** in June from **4.3%** in May. The labor market remains resilient, defying recession fears. This gives the Fed cover to hold rates steady.

### Fed Funds Rate
Steady at **3.63%**. No changes over the window.

Traded at **4.57%** as of July 16, down **10 bps** from 4.67% on May 19. The downtrend in yields reflects the softening CPI data and expectations of a peak in the rate cycle.
