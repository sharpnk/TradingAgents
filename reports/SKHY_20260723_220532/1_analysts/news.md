### 1.2 ADR Arbitrage — A Unique Structural Feature

**51% Premium with Strict Conversion Cap**
- SK Hynix's ADR (SKHY) trades at a **~51% premium** to its Korean-listed ordinary shares (000660.KS). The ADR cap remains in place after the $26.5 billion ADR sale, restricting new ADR creation.
- This means the ADR premium is "locked in" by the conversion limit — a double-edged sword:
  - **Bullish for SKHY holders:** Premium can persist or widen further
  - **Risk:** Any cap relaxation could trigger a sharp premium mean-reversion
