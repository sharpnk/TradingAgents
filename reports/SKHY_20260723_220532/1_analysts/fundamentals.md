## 6. STRATEGIC & SECTOR CONTEXT

### The AI / HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) Tailwind
SK hynix is the **dominant player in HBM3/HBM3E memory**, critical for NVIDIA and other AI GPU accelerators. The explosive revenue growth from ₩32.8T (2023) → ₩97.1T (2025) → projected >₩200T (2026) is directly attributable to AI-driven memory demand.

### Cyclical Recovery
After a severe downcycle in 2023 (operating loss of ₩7.7T), the cyclical recovery in memory pricing began in late 2023 and accelerated through 2024-2025. The company's profitability has rebounded far beyond previous peak levels.

### CapEx Intensity
- CapEx of ₩28.6T in 2025 (+71% YoY) indicates aggressive capacity expansion
- Q1 2026 CapEx of ₩7.9T suggests another ₩30T+ year is likely
- This positions SK hynix for continued leadership in advanced memory production
