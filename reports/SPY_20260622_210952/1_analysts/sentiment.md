## 6. Conclusion

The sentiment picture for SPY over the June 15–22 period is **Mildly Bearish**. The dominant catalyst is the new Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish policy meeting, which shocked markets and spiked rate-hike odds. This is consistently reflected across all three sources — news, retail chat, and Reddit — making it the strongest signal in the dataset.

Tech weakness (SpaceX -16%, megacap selling, Dan Niles' cautious call on hyperscalers) reinforces the bearish tilt. StockTwits retail traders are clearly more bearish than bullish (27% vs 20%, with unlabeled posts leaning negative), and the Reddit community shows scattered frustration.

The Goldilocks economy narrative and JPMorgan's 8,900 bull case provide a counter-balancing floor, preventing a fully Bearish assessment. The PCE release this week and US-Iran diplomatic progress are near-term catalysts that could shift sentiment.

**Key takeaway for traders:** Sentiment is cautious with a bearish bias. Institutional news still carries constructive long-term framing (earnings-driven rally), but retail has turned fearful and the Fed is now a headwind. Monitor PCE data and any de-escalation in Iran talks as potential sentiment reversals. The divergence between optimistic institutions and fearful retail traders often resolves with volatility.
