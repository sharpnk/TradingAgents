## 1. Company Profile & Background

**SPY** (State Street SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust) is the oldest and most heavily traded ETF in the world, launched in 1993. It is structured as a unit investment trust that holds the 500 largest publicly traded U.S. companies, mirroring the composition and performance of the S&P 500 Index. The trust is managed by State Street Global Advisors (SSGA), and its primary objective is to provide investment results that, before expenses, correspond generally to the price and yield performance of the S&P 500 Index.
